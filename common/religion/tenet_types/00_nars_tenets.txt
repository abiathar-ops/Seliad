# Sixteen Tenets: three diverging paths (Narses = Learning, Phokas = Stewardship, Enuo = Martial),
# five themed tenets each, plus one Mega Tenet for the Endgame Faith that fuses the best of the other 15.
#
# SYMBIOSES: each pairing feeds the other. [+Sx] marks parameters added for that pairing.
# S1  Stress <-> Seclusion          sanguine: learning_per_stress_level makes stress fuel, losing stress grants piety
# S2  Condemn <-> Revoke <-> Clemency   pantheon + erudition: condemned vassals lose titles tyranny-free, sparing them pays fulfillment
# S3  Temples <-> Holy sites        pantheon + erudition: temples give development and piety, holy sites and pilgrim circuits give prosperity
# S4  Study <-> Scholar             records + university: scholar trait sources feed scholasticism_is_holy
# S5  Gold <-> Piety flywheel       banking + patronage: donations buy piety, piety raises tax and stewardship
# S6  Craft <-> Sacrifice           artisans: amphora decision supplies cheap artifacts to destroy for piety
# S7  Culture <-> Conversion <-> Calm   statecraft + tolerance: convert fast, keep vassals quiet, minorities pay
# S8  Peace pays                    tolerance piety_from_long_peace -> banking tax per piety level
# S9  Kill <-> Trophy <-> Piety     crusade + warlike: kills give heads, heads give piety, piety gives prowess
# S10 Seduction <-> Fertility <-> Children   deviance + vestals: sensual traits raise fertility, births give piety, hooks and gold
# S11 Siege <-> Capture <-> Ransom or Execute   spoils (+ patronage ransom captives)
# S12 Dread <-> Piety               spoils: executions and sieges build dread, dread gives piety and knight strength
